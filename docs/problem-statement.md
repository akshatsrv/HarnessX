# Problem Statement: ClaimGuard AI

## Pitch Deck Summary
> Build an AI-powered insurance claims investigation assistant that combines document intelligence, historical claims analytics, policy retrieval and explainable AI reasoning to identify claims requiring additional review, summarize supporting evidence, and recommend the next best action to a human investigator.

## Detailed Challenges in Modern Claims Processing

Insurance companies process claims that require synthesizing information from disparate sources:
1. **Manual Effort:** Evidence is fragmented across claims forms, police reports, repair invoices, policy schedules, customer histories, and internal investigation guidelines.
2. **Investigation Latency:** Claims analysts spend hours gathering and correlating data before assessing risk.
3. **Inconsistent Assessments:** Similar evidence is interpreted subjectively across different claims handlers.
4. **Black-box Risk Scores:** Traditional fraud detection models output a numeric score without explaining *why* a claim was flagged or citing verifiable evidence.
5. **Missed Relationships:** Repeat claim patterns, inflated repair line-items, or multi-claimant collusion are hard to detect manually.
6. **Policy Ambiguity:** Exclusions, deductible tiers, and inspection requirements are buried across extensive documentation.
7. **Weak Audit Trail:** Notes, reasoning, and evidence are stored haphazardly, risking regulatory non-compliance.

ClaimGuard AI bridges this gap with an explainable, human-in-the-loop decision-support system.
