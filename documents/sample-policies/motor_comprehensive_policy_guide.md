# Comprehensive Private Car Insurance Policy & Investigation Guidelines (Ref: POL-MOT-2026-V1)

## Section 4: Claims Processing & Damage Verification

### 4.1 Mandatory Physical Survey & Inspection
Any private motor insurance claim where the estimated repair cost exceeds ₹2,50,000, or where structural damage to the bonnet, chassis, or engine bay is reported, **shall require an in-person physical survey** by an IRDAI-licensed independent surveyor prior to settlement authorization.

### 4.2 Document Date & Location Consistency
All submitted documentation—including the claim intimation form, Police First Information Report (FIR) / incident dairy entry, and initial workshop repair estimate—must present consistent dates of incident and geographical locations. 
- In the event of an unexplained temporal discrepancy exceeding 24 hours between the stated claim incident date and the police incident record, claim fast-track approval is suspended.
- The case **must be escalated for manual claims investigator review** to verify chronological validity.

### 4.3 Repair Cost Baselines and Line-Item Variance
When a submitted repair estimate exceeds the regional model-specific baseline for comparable collision damage categories by more than 35%, workshop labor rates and parts replacement line-items must be audited against authorized OEM catalog prices.
